ABOUT 2A

Markets move.
People react.
We research both.

2A Research is an independent, student-led research organisation. We build open data tools and original research on financial markets, digital assets and investor behaviour, for a new generation of investors.

WHY WE EXIST

Clearer information for new investors.

Young people now meet markets through apps, social media and crypto exchanges long before they meet a textbook. Much of what reaches them is built to sell, not to explain.

We want to offer the opposite: measurements that publish their method, research that shows its data, and findings stated with their limits.

HOW WE WORK

Measure. Question. Research. Publish.

Independent

We are not part of any exchange, broker or token project. Any sponsorship or partnership will be disclosed on the page it relates to.

Student-led

2A Research is run by students. It is currently an independent initiative and is not a registered company or charity.

Not advice

Nothing we publish is investment advice. Our indices describe market conditions; they do not predict prices or tell anyone what to buy or sell.
